# Before the Superstore revision — 6 November 2026

Historical snapshot of the three lessons and three practicals before the user
authorized new content throughout the course. The originals' assets, datasets
and conversion sources remain in Git and the untouched reference decks remain
under sources/slides/. These notebooks are historical records, not active render
targets. Their relative paths reflect their former location and are not intended
as a second runnable course project.

The active lessons now use Superstore, COLA and Consumer. Conversion scripts
export historical slide conversions under _build/reference-conversions/ and do
not overwrite the authored active notebooks. Funds and cereals are no longer
part of student publishing or downloads.
